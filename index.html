Juggle Legend is a 3D football juggling game for Android: one tap at the right moment keeps the ball up. Start in the backyard and juggle your way to a World Cup final.

  • ⚽ Level up to unlock knees, chest, headers, flicks, stalls and around the world
  • 🗺️ 6 venues from the Backyard to a World Cup night final, with wind, pigeons and dogs
  • ⭐ 30 challenges, 49 trophies, missions and a Daily Juggle
  • 👻 Race the ghost of your best run
  • 🏀 Plus 5 more silly games with 100 levels: dunk into hippos, bonk with your head...
  • ✈️ Free and plays offline

Dress your player

Pick kits, boots and hair colors, put your own number and name on the shirt, and juggle a tennis ball, a melon or an egg.
